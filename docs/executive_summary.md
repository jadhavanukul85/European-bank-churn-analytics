# Executive Summary: European Banking Churn & Capital Exposure Analysis

**Prepared For:** Executive Board & Regulatory Oversight Stakeholders  
**Focus Area:** Retail Capital Retention & Systemic Risk Reduction  

### Key Empirical Takeaways

1. **Systemic Concentration of Churn:** Churn is not evenly distributed; it is highly concentrated in specific client segments.
2. **Geographic Risk Asymmetry:** **Germany** exhibits higher attrition rates than France and Spain, representing a regional capital flight risk.
3. **High-Value Client Attrition:** Churn among clients in the **46–60 age group** and **High-Balance segments** is double the systemic average, directly eroding bank core deposits.
4. **Product Friction Anomaly:** Customers with 3 or more bank products display an anomalous churn rate, pointing to service integration issues or aggressive product bundling.

### Strategic Recommendations

* **Immediate Governance Review:** Re-evaluate current multi-product cross-selling incentives to prevent customer dissatisfaction and churn.
* **Targeted Deposit Protection:** Focus retention campaigns on high-balance accounts held by clients aged 40–60.
* **Digital Interface Optimization:** Transition passive/inactive account holders into active digital users via personalized financial management tools.
